      The test

      How studying here works, and what to expect from the FAA's Part 107 knowledge test.

      How this site works

      1. Pick a set of study cards. 10, 30, 60, or 100 questions, from every subject or just one. Cards from your last session are held back, so sets stay fresh.
      2. Answer and check. Miss once and you get one more try. Miss twice and the answer appears with an explanation and the FAA source behind it.
      3. Review your misses. Anything you miss goes on your review list until you get it right on the first try.
      4. Run a full practice exam. Exam simulation gives you 60 questions and a two-hour clock, scored against the same 70% the FAA uses.

          Privacy

          Without an account, everything stays in your browser. Nothing about your studying is sent anywhere.

          With an account, your name, email address, and study progress (answers, review list, streak, and settings) are stored in Google Firebase so you can sign in on another device and pick up where you left off.

          Your data is only used to run this site. It isn't sold, shared, or used for ads, and there are no trackers.

          The site's admins can see the list of accounts: name, email address, and when each person last used the site. They cannot see your answers or progress.

          You can delete your account and all of its saved data at any time in Settings → Account.
